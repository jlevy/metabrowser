Asked by the user, as a design convention: a toggle that expands a diff summary (the Pulls tab's rows in the nav column, the top of the PR page) puts its chevron after the summary (+123 −456, or the variant with file statuses such as 3 A, 1 M). Folder toggles and stack toggles keep their chevron in front. The design system states the convention.
