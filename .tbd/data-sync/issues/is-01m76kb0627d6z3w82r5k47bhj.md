The file summary at the top of a folder's Overview is too long to be mandatory. Show the population table, the 'Share of ...' caption, and a GitHub Languages-style legend (dot, name, share) of up to five largest file-type families plus Other; put the full grouped table, families, and N more rows behind a Details toggle that reuses the N more disclosure (section-disclosure-trigger). Rule: families only, from every group, ranked by the selected measure in the caption's population, above the 1% share floor, at most five; the rest is one neutral Other entry. Details starts closed per mount, persists through repaints of the mount, is not a preference.
