Enforce owner-only repository and provider cache storage: 0700 directories and 0600 files on POSIX and an equivalent current-user-only ACL on Windows. Refuse remote acquisition or provider publication through symlinked, foreign-owned, group/world-accessible, or unverifiable cache ancestors, including explicit METABROWSER_HOME; do not merely warn and write. Preserve ordinary read-only local browsing outside the application home. Test umask, repair/refusal, private sources, and actionable diagnostics.
