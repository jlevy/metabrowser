The Overview row renders an empty number span, so its title sits out at the title column with no heading above it and reads as indented. A step outside any group (a flat guide, or a step between groups) likewise reads as indented relative to the group headings. A row outside a group should start on the edge the group headings start on; rows inside a group keep their numbered column under their heading.
