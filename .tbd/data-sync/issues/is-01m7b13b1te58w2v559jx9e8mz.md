The user, 2026-11-13: on a PR page, switching tabs (Conversation, Changes, Checks) makes the author's profile icon flicker. Likely cause: paintHeader -> paintStateRow rebuilds the identity with replaceChildren, and authorLabel creates a fresh <img loading=lazy decoding=async> each time, which paints blank until it decodes even when cached. authorLabel also builds timeline, comment, review, and Pulls-list avatars, so other repaints may flicker too. Fix: don't rebuild unchanged nodes (skip unchanged state-row paints, or reuse identity/img nodes keyed by URL), and decode before swapping when an image must change; sweep for every repaint that recreates images or icons whose inputs didn't change; add a test that catches a recreated unchanged image. Coordinate with the perf session's #370 (header layout).
