## Notes

2026-11-15, from PR #473 review A5 (https://github.com/jlevy/metabrowser/pull/473#pullrequestreview-5915676394): the suspected race was the fixed 450 ms wait in TREE_TIPS (tests/dom/files-tab-chrome.mjs) against the 300 ms tooltip timer (TOOLTIP_APPEAR_DELAY, app.js). Tested on claude/group-heading-tally: replacing the sleep with a poll on the tooltip's display (up to 5 s) did NOT fix it. With the poll alone, 6 of 18 local runs at load average 4 to 14 still failed, 5 of them read as treeTips file visible False with the tooltip never showing within 5 s (the sixth was not inspected). A focusin recorder was in the last 8 of those runs; in all 3 failures among them it caught focus moving during the file read, and in 2 it named the target, the Group by trigger (BUTTON chip chip-menu-trigger disclosure-toggle), landing inside the 300 ms delay. Ungrouping is chosen from the Group by menu, so focus is in the filter bar; the bar's rebuild when the tree's tallies answer puts focus back on the trigger (filter-controls.js, the focus restore near lines 690-709), and document focusin -> hideTooltip (app.js, near line 3345) cancels the pending tooltip. Test fix on #473: TREE_TIPS and the new SECTION_TIPS move focus to a tree row first (the restore leaves focus outside the bar alone) and poll the tooltip instead of sleeping; TREE_TIPS keeps the focusin recorder in its result (treeTips.why) for any later failure's message. After the fix, 4 of 4 runs passed at load average 10 to 14, then 3 of 3 at load average about 10 (7 of 7 in all). Fix commit 4d659021c on claude/group-heading-tally. Not closed: CI at the new head is the evidence the coordinator weighs. Open product question, not filed: a programmatic focus restore in the bar also cancels a real pointer's tooltip on the tree.
