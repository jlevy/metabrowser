cli-api-nav.tryscript.md pins /api/rollup with truncated=false and says explicitly that the true case is out of reach: max_files is a hard-coded 500000 in settings.py with no request parameter to lower it, so a truncated rollup needs a generated corpus rather than a sandbox fixture. Two ways to close it: add a bounded request parameter for max_files (which would also be useful to the browser), or drive the case from devtools/bench_serving.py's corpus builder in a non-golden test. Until then the truncated branch of the rollup projection is untested end to end.
