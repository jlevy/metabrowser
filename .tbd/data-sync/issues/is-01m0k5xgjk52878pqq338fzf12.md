Phase 3 of the load-time plan. Measured by hand on 2026-08-21 on a 998,560-file synthetic tree that was not build_corpus (the walker stopped at 500,000): the warm root /api/tree took 4,990 ms and returned 3,676,480 B, on the route that answered in 9.4 ms while scanning. The cost was serving the whole known tree, not the route.

Not re-measured since the inventory engine (#74) replaced the index and the tree projection, which now reads pages of INVENTORY_TREE_PAGE_ROWS = 4,096 rows (settings.py). Re-measure on the current build at the cap before choosing a fix. H25 (mb-43v7, an encoded-body cache and validator) and H45 (mb-0l4a, orjson) are candidate parts of one.
