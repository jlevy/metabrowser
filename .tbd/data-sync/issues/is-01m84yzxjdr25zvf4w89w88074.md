Review B1 (Medium) of PR #761: review B on the pull request (link added once posted) (review B, follow-up, round 2, head 0ace3fe37).

After a rate limit lifts, a cores walk held for the catalog's backfill is answered `held` with the lapsed `rate_limited` failure, and the Pulls panel then retries every second instead of backing off to SLOW_POLL_MS, for the rest of the backfill.

Mechanism:

- request_preparation copies a failed list stamp into pulls_set.core_failure (pull_cores.py:609-625). _failure_held stops holding a rate_limited failure at its reset_at (pull_cores.py:315-322), but core_failure is cleared only when a walk batch succeeds (pull_cores.py:505), on a bind, or for a new catalog identity (served_pull.py:1174, :1201).
- Once the backfill reads its next page, the stamp is succeeded and the catalog is not done, so _backfill_first holds the walk. The preparation route answers every `held` with core_failure (sidekick.py:660-666), the lapsed rate limit.
- pauseCoreQueue waits Math.max(CORE_BUSY_RETRY_MS, reset - now) whenever reset_at parses (pulls-panel.js:1044-1047): one second once the reset has passed. warmRetryDelay doubles but is not read in that branch. Each retry also polls the list (poll(true), :1055).

Evidence (scratch copies, never the tree):

- A copy of tests/test_github_pulls.py on the A2 test's stand: with the clock moved past reset_at and the stamp back to succeeded on an unfinished catalog, a background ask answers {'preparation': 'held', 'failure': {'outcome': 'rate_limited', 'message': 'limit', 'reset_at': '2026-09-17T12:00:02Z'}} at 12:00:03.
- A copy of tests/dom/github-pr-prefetch-behavior.js's rig answering every demand held for 120 virtual seconds, driving the production pulls-panel.js: with failure null or a stale network_error, 8 asks (gaps 4, 8, 16, 30, 30 s); with a rate_limited failure whose reset_at was 60 s past, 119 asks a second apart.

Reach: a rate limit during a first visit's backfill or a new epoch's; the loop lasts for the rest of the backfill (seconds for a few thousand rows, about two hours at 20,000 in review A's model), unless a batch of rows on screen succeeds. Before #761 the same loop followed a share hold with a stale failure, up to the hour's roll. Each round is local (GET pulls with an entity tag, POST pull-cores-prepare); no GitHub call.

Fix (pick one): (1) in pauseCoreQueue use the reset only while it is ahead, `Number.isFinite(reset) && reset > deps.now() ? Math.max(CORE_BUSY_RETRY_MS, reset - deps.now()) : warmRetryDelay`; (2) attach `failure` to a held answer only while _failure_held holds it; (3) clear core_failure once _failure_held no longer holds it. Fix (1), tried on a scratch copy of the plugin's scripts, gives 8 asks in 120 s for the lapsed rate limit (gaps 4, 8, 16, 30, 30 s), and the production github-pr-prefetch-behavior.js session gives the facts test_github_pulls_panel_session.py pins on that copy; (2) and (3) were not tried. Pin it with a panel session in which a held answer with a past reset_at backs off to SLOW_POLL_MS, or a production-path test that a walk held for the backfill after a lifted limit carries no lapsed failure.
