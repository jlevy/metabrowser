Before T5 fixes its loading behavior, measure in headless Chrome (not the hidden browser
pane) on the 1,031-file comparison used for review of #284 and on a small one:

- the narrowed comparison route's server time and transfer, cold and cached;
- remounting the file list for a narrowed answer, through first screen;
- the same when the narrowed answer was prefetched on hover/focus of a menu row.

Target: no visible spinner, and under about 50 ms to a drawn first screen for a cached
or prefetched answer (the "effortlessly fast" principle). Record the numbers beside the
constant or prefetch decision they set. If a refetch cannot meet the target, bring the
measurements back to the epic before choosing another design.
