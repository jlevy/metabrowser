A spinner beside text like 'Loading folder…' or 'Loading file…' is clunky and redundant — the context already says what is loading. Show the spinner alone (keeping an sr-only/aria label for screen readers) and encode this in the design system. Distinct non-generic states (e.g. 'Still scanning this folder…') may keep their explanatory copy.
