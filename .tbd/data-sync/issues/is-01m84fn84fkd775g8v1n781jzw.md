A nested folder's header strip and frame take its dominant extension's color, but a folder usually holds many file types, so the color claims one type for all of them. That color also counts every file, even with Show ignored off. Draw a nested folder's header and frame in a neutral surface color, and stop marking those folders in a family hover. Decide separately whether a folder drawn as one solid cell, with its contents not drawn, keeps its dominant color. Reported in manual testing of the stack.
