The trusted-publishing workflow currently constructs release artifacts in the same job that receives PyPI OIDC authority. Split artifact construction and full verification into an unprivileged job, pass the immutable artifacts through the CI artifact boundary, and give only the final publish job the OIDC permission. Preserve tag/version attestation and verify hashes before upload. This is defense in depth, not a blocker for the reviewed 0.9.2 candidate.
