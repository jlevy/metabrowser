The trusted-publishing workflow, .github/workflows/publish.yml, builds, verifies and publishes in one job, build-and-publish, which holds id-token: write throughout. Split artifact construction and full verification into an unprivileged job, pass the immutable artifacts through the CI artifact boundary, and give only the final publish job the OIDC permission. Preserve tag/version attestation and verify hashes before upload. Defense in depth, not a release blocker.
