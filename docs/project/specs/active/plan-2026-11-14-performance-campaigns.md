# Plan: The Next Performance Campaigns

**Date:** 2026-11-14

**Author:** Joshua Levy (github.com/jlevy) with LLM assistance

**Status:** Draft.
The lanes and readiness below are current; the priority order waits on
the architecture review in [Open Questions](#open-questions).

## Overview

exp-079 closed Phase 1 of the November 11 campaign (epic `mb-mpo0`): every accepted
change measured together at final `main` against the campaign control, with a
[Final Performance Update](../../../../explorations/performance-loop/experiments/exp-079-every-accepted-change-at-final-main.md#final-performance-update)
for each hypothesis.
Nothing measured moves during a transition any more.
The pull request page paints later than it did, because it now holds its first paint
until the content is final.

Two families of work remain.
The first is Phases 2 and 3 of the same campaign, H90 and H93 to H111, from
[instant transitions and stable layout](plan-2026-11-11-ui-apparent-performance.md).
The second is the older load-time backlog in
[the load-time plan’s registry](plan-2026-08-21-load-time-performance.md#hypotheses).
It has been worked one row at a time since exp-010 and was never sorted into loops.
This plan sorts both into lanes, records what each hypothesis still needs before its
first timed run, and gives the order to work them in.
The registry stays the one place each hypothesis is defined.
This plan only orders them and names what blocks each one.

## Goals

- Every open hypothesis meets the readiness standard below before its first timed run,
  and its blocker is named until then.
- Every lane runs under the
  [runbook](../../../../explorations/performance-loop/README.md): a committed protocol
  named by hash, a paced series, the accept rule, and a record whatever the outcome.
- Phases 2 and 3 end in their own closing round, in Chrome and in `WKWebView`, with a
  Final Performance Update for every hypothesis they land.
- A fresh agent can pick up any lane from this document and the runbook alone.

## Non-Goals

- Native shell code in `apps/macos`. The shell half of H97, prewarming, and the sticky
  port stay deferred to the native app plan’s beads (`mb-25nv`, `mb-w4kd`).
- Small tuning. A lane earns its place by a cost the measurements show, not by a change
  that is merely possible.

## Background

### What stands

- **The loop.** [The runbook](../../../../explorations/performance-loop/README.md) holds
  the round steps, the accept rule with its four clauses, pre-registration, and where a
  round’s files live. [Durable Storage](../../../development.md#durable-storage) says
  where each kind of file and lock belongs.
- **The instrument.** The transition scenario at revision 5, frozen at `3a1ff25e`, with
  the `empty`, `newest`, and `restored` Commits landings.
- **The corpus.** Corpus v2, checked out at `7a02fed4`, trunk `083e78e4`, refs digest
  `387f68dc4e8eb8e0`. Nothing fetches inside it.
- **The control.** `main` at `09903292`, the exp-058 reference.
- **The driver.**
  [paced-series.sh](../../../../explorations/performance-loop/paced-series.sh) runs a
  judged series one pair at a time, with the series lock, the load, power and corpus
  gates, and the stop rules the runbook’s “A judged series” describes.
- **The ledger.** [report.md](../../../../explorations/performance-loop/report.md),
  regenerated by `run.py report`.

### The readiness standard

A hypothesis is ready for its first timed run when its registry row and its bead give
all of these:

1. a claim that can come out false;
2. its deciding metric or metrics, with the predicted direction;
3. its guards, including the metrics earlier rounds accepted on that it could undo;
4. the instrument that reads them, by path, which exists today;
5. the regime: browser and engine, corpus, cold or warm, power and load;
6. an open bead that matches the row;
7. a reserved experiment id that collides with nothing;
8. its dependencies on other hypotheses and beads;
9. what must exist first: code, an instrument fix, or the user at an unlocked screen for
   `WKWebView`.

On 2026-11-14 no open hypothesis met all nine.
Most lack only items 4 and 9, an instrument reading or the code itself.

## Design

### Lanes of the UI campaign

Timed Chrome series run one at a time under the series lock, so a lane is a lane of
development, not of measurement.
Its timed series still queue behind the others.
`WKWebView` runs take their own probe lock and need the user present.

| Lane | What | Rows and beads, in order | Gated on |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Instruments | Recorder revision 6: `mb-86b9`, `mb-5xzz`, `mb-s0e4`, a retained-memory reading, a dwell journey, a time-to-highlighted milestone; then `compare --aa` on `main` (`mb-tpol`) | Nothing |
| A | Attribution | `mb-0t7d` and `mb-f8g9` as one series over the four lane merges; `mb-i9vb`’s mechanism against `commits.cold.1` | Nothing; runs on revision 5 |
| B | Background and prediction | H90’s re-test (`mb-mbd3`), then H93, H94, H108, H104 | H90 and H93 before H94; H93 caps the requests the rest add |
| C | Retention | H95, then H98, H99 (a), H105 | Lane 0; H90 for H98 |
| D | Landing | `mb-mjc0`’s waterfall, then H109 or H110, then H96, then H103 | One cost, so one sequence |
| E | Render cost | H100, H102, then H99 (b) before H101 | H99 (b) and H101 cut the same task |
| W | `WKWebView` | `mb-o3hr`, `mb-32en`’s WebKit half, `mb-8k5g`, then H97 | The user present; batch it |
| Close | Phase 2–3 closing round | `mb-vmb4` | Every lane above that lands |

Rounds that compete for the same cost, and so must not be judged against one another’s
control:

- **Cold landing:** H96, H109, H110, and H103. H103 changes how
  `startup_script_transfer_kb` is measured.
- **Retained revisits:** H95, H98, H99 (a), and H105 share one memory budget, and H100’s
  share of a revisit shrinks once surfaces are retained.
- **The large source view:** H99 (b) and H101.
- **Background requests:** H93 governs the requests H94, H108, and H104 add.

### Loops of the load-time backlog

The load-time audit of 2026-11-14 found no row from H5 to H57 ready: none had a reserved
id, named guards, or a stated regime, and several cited retired corpora.
It sorted the live rows into four loops, ranked for the native app, where every window
open pays for the first and the fourth is off the app’s path.

| Rank | Loop | Order | Build first |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A folder’s first frame, and server start | H56, H54, H52; alongside, H14 then H17; H57’s open half runs as H103 then H96 | A `visual_states` counter; a deep-against-shallow spawn pair; startup pairs with cached bytecode |
| 2 | Scan and walker | H39, then H40 as fdu provider adoption (`mb-hej8`); H18, H72, H22; H33, H43; H34, H47 | A gitignore toggle in `scan_bench.py`; an executor-size knob; a “tracked indexed” metric |
| 3 | Revisit and persistence | H49, then H21, H46, H42 | Warm-reopen and server-memory adapters; a persisted-format load-cost prototype |
| 4 | Remote link, `metab --remote` only | H44, H29, H25, H45 | A prefetch-total metric; a batched `/api/trees` prototype |

Loop 3 has the highest ceiling for relaunch, but only H49 can show whether re-walks stay
common once warm folders land.
The registry rows carry each hypothesis’s current status, and the rows the audit found
overtaken are retired or merged there.

## Implementation Plan

### Order of work

1. **Lane 0 and Lane A first, together.** Lane A runs on revision 5 now, so exp-079’s
   unexplained wrong-way results are pinned on their causes before H108 tries to win the
   pull request page’s time back.
   Lane 0 is code with no timed series until its A/A run.
2. **Then the first improvement lanes:** Lane B (H90’s re-test, H93, then H108) and Lane
   D (the waterfall), and load-time Loop 1, in that order of timed series.
3. **Then Lane C** once revision 6 is frozen, and Lane E.
4. **Lane W** whenever the user can be at the machine, with everything waiting on
   `WKWebView` batched into that session.
5. **The Phase 2–3 closing round** (`mb-vmb4`) once the lanes that are going to land
   have landed.

The architecture review may reorder steps 2 and 3. Its findings go into the registry as
rows and into this list as a revision, not as a second plan.

### Picking up a lane as a fresh agent

1. Read the runbook’s “Running a round”, “Where a round’s files live”, “A judged
   series”, and “The accept rule”, then
   [Durable Storage](../../../development.md#durable-storage).
2. Read the hypothesis’s registry row and its bead, then the records it cites.
3. Write the protocol as
   `explorations/performance-loop/experiments/exp-NNN-protocol.txt` with the analysis
   and exclusion scripts named by sha256, and commit and push it before the first timed
   pair.
4. Copy
   [paced-series.example.env](../../../../explorations/performance-loop/paced-series.example.env)
   to the round’s gitignored `attic/expNNN/series.env`, fill it in, and run
   `paced-series.sh --config attic/expNNN/series.env --check` until every gate passes.
5. Hand the series start to the coordinator.
   The operator judges clauses 1 to 3 mechanically with the registered analysis; the
   coordinator writes clause 4.
6. Write the record whatever the outcome, regenerate the ledger, update the registry row
   and the bead, and open the pull request.

The next free experiment id and hypothesis number are the ones after the highest the
registry and `experiments/` use.
Check with `git grep` and `git log --all -S` across fetched branches before reserving,
since an open pull request can hold one.

## Testing Strategy

The plan itself is checked by the storage test for every record it leads to
(`tests/test_experiment_storage.py`) and by the parity and link checks `make verify`
runs. Each lane’s rounds are tested the way the runbook requires: an instrument change
fails its own definition tests before it measures anything, and a round’s record is
regenerated from its committed samples.

## Open Questions

- **The architecture review.** A deeper review is to ask whether the design is built for
  the best performance it can reach, not which small changes remain: where the shell,
  the server, and the client tree divide the work; which of those divisions forces cost
  on every transition; and which uncertainties deserve a measurement before anyone
  builds. Its findings become registry rows in the standard form and may reorder the
  lanes.
- **WebKit time.** Lane W needs the user at an unlocked screen.
  When that session happens decides when H97 and the Phase 2–3 closing round can finish.
