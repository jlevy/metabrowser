# Load-Time Exploration Ledger

Generated by `explorations/performance-loop/run.py report` from the recorded runs in
[results/runs.jsonl](results/runs.jsonl) and the artifacts in
[experiments/](experiments/). Do not edit by hand.

Each experiment is one soft-schema artifact: validated YAML front matter carries the
measured numbers and the verdict, and the Markdown body carries the reasoning a schema
cannot hold. How a round is run, and the rule that decides whether a change is kept:
[the loop’s README](README.md).
