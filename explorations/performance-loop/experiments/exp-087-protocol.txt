- Judged pairs: those that pass the load rule and have a power mode.
- Judged mode: the first power mode to reach 10 judged pairs. If the series ends with no mode at 10,
  it is the mode with the most judged pairs, provided it has at least 6; with fewer, the round is
  reported and not decided. Pairs in other modes are kept in the samples, unjudged. Modes are never
  pooled.

Deciding question (clause 3 for H87). For each view V in files.cold.json, files.cold.image and
files.revisit.markdown_large:
- M(V) is the modal framesToReady over all judged runs of both builds for V; a tie takes the smaller
  count.
- A run lands on the later frame when framesToReady > M(V).
- Build the 2x2 table: candidate later or not, against control later or not.
- Compute the two-sided Fisher exact p-value: the sum of the hypergeometric probabilities of every
  table with the observed margins whose probability is at most the observed table's, with a
  relative tolerance of 1e-7.
- A view differs beyond chance when p < 0.05. There is no correction for testing three views.
files.revisit.markdown_large is added to the two views named in the request, so that all ten of
exp-083's wrong-way results are covered.
Decision:
- If no view differs beyond chance, exp-083's wrong-way results on these views (5, 4, and 1) are
  accounted for as frame-quantization flips, and H87 is accepted on exp-083's metric and guard (43
  to 1 conditional requests per reload; reload painted-ready 158.1 to 124.7 ms).
- If any view differs, H87 stays unresolved, and the record gives each view's rates (later/judged
  per build) and p.
