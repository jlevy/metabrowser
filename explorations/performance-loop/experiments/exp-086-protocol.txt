LOCK, LOAD, POWER, PACING
- Take the series lock behind whoever holds it, after the one-minute load has been under
  12 for two minutes and no `transitions.mjs run` process exists; hold it across every
  paced pair and the server half; release it when the last ends, however it ends.
- Paced pairs: one `transitions.mjs run --runs 1` invocation per pair, the two runs back
  to back; the control's --condition first in odd-numbered paced pairs and the
  candidate's first in even ones; each pair starts only once the one-minute load has
  stayed under 12 for 60 s, sampled every 10 s.
- Load rule, from load fields only and before any metric is read: a pair is excluded if
  either run's one-minute load is above 12 at its start or at the start of any measured
  transition.
- Power rule: Low Power Mode is sampled every 15 s with timestamps. A pair is excluded if
  the mode changes during either run or differs between its runs. If lowpowermode reads 0
  when the first paced pair starts, the round runs in that mode, otherwise in 1; judged
  pairs never mix modes, and pairs in the other mode are reported, not judged.
- Target 6 judged pairs; at most 20 paced pairs attempted. Fewer than 6 judged after 20:
  judged as they are if at least 3, said so; fewer than 3: no verdict.
- Excluded rows are kept and recorded with their reason.

IDENTITY-PERIOD EXPLANATION CHECK (declared now; not accounting: no guard is judged on a
subset because of it, and it decides no verdict)
- From each judged run's server log, the git-backed requests are those whose path starts
  with /api/git/ or /api/plugin/diff/. In arrival order on that server, the first is a
  TTL boundary, and so is each later one arriving at least 5,000 ms after the previous
  boundary's arrival. Every other git-backed request is within the TTL.
- For /api/plugin/diff/comparison and /api/git/commit/, pooled over the judged runs of
  each build (warm-up and measured passes), the median server duration (the log's ms) of
  boundary requests is divided by the median of within-TTL requests of the same route.
- The check FITS the identity account if that ratio is at least 1.3 on the control for
  both routes and below 1.3 on the candidate for both routes; it does NOT FIT otherwise,
  and is reported either way, with the counts.
- Also recorded per judged run: for commits.hover_click and commits.cold.1, whether the
  transition's comparison request (exp-086 registration 1's rule: hover, the last GET of
  .../comparison?revision=dbfd82b9...; cold.1, the last GET of ...revision=3669a358...
  that arrived before the last GET of ...revision=083af6f8...) is a TTL boundary, and
  its server duration.
