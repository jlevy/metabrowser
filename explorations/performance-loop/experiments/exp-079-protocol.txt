Series
- transitions.mjs run --exp exp-079 --runs 1, every journey (no --journeys), the standard
  regime, one back-to-back pair per invocation, the leading condition alternating, and fresh
  state directories for exp-079.
- Pacing: a pair starts only after the one-minute load has stayed under 12 for 60 s and no other
  transitions.mjs run is in progress (as in exp-087).
- Lock: $(git rev-parse --path-format=absolute --git-common-dir)/metabrowser-series.lock, taken through the same gate. It is held through every timed run and
  released when the series ends, however it ends.
- Load rule: as in exp-060 and exp-087 (exp-060-062-load-filter.mjs). A pair is excluded if any
  run in it saw a load above 12 at its start or at any measured transition's start, or if it is
  incomplete.
- Power rule: as in exp-087 (lowpowermode and power source read before and after each pair; all
  four must agree).
  - The judged mode is the mode read before the first attempt. Low Power Mode off is preferred,
    but the round runs in whatever the first attempt reads, and the record names it.
  - Pairs in any other mode are excluded and never pooled.
- Target: 6 judged pairs in the judged mode, at most 20 attempts. With fewer than 6 when the
  attempts run out, the round is decided on at least 4; with fewer than 4 it is reported and not
  decided.
