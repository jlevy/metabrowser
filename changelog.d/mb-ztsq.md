- A repository’s first visit fills its pull-request catalog first, as fast as GitHub
  answers within the background’s share of the hour, so Quick Search across every state
  and the ref chooser’s pull requests by branch are complete once it is read.
  The descriptions of rows not on screen follow, taking what the background prefetch
  leaves, which keeps a scan’s records while a Pulls tab is open; the rows on screen are
  described at once, as before.
  On a repository with many thousands of pull requests, whose first fill outlasts the
  background’s share of the hour, the fill comes first: until it is done, the background
  prefetch and the first read of label colors get only what it leaves.
