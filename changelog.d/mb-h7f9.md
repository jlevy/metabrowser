- **Lean is a known language.** `.lean` and `.hlean` files are the Lean family, in
  GitHub’s Lean blue, across the Files filter, Overview, the Treemap, and file icons,
  and their Source view is highlighted by the
  [highlightjs-lean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/highlightjs-lean) grammar,
  with `sorry` and `admit` marked as warnings.
  [Adding a Language](docs/adding-a-language.md) is the recipe for the next one.
