- **A pull request’s fields read in one order.** Its title, its place in its stack, its
  number, and its author follow one another in that order on a Pulls row, in the stack
  menu, on a stack’s page, in the ref selector, and in Quick Search.
  A ref selector row’s pull request line now reads its title before its number, and
  Quick Search says a pull request’s title before its number to a screen reader.
  A Pulls row, the stack menu, and a stack’s page draw the fields with one builder
  ([One Order for a Pull Request’s Fields](docs/design-system.md#one-order-for-a-pull-requests-fields)).
