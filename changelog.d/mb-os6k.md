- **A pull request’s fields read in one order everywhere.** Its title, its place in its
  stack, its number, and its author follow one another in that order on every row that
  shows them. A ref selector row’s pull request line now reads its title before its
  number, and Quick Search says a pull request’s title before its number to a screen
  reader. A Pulls row, the stack menu, and a stack’s page draw the fields with one
  builder
  ([One Order for a Pull Request’s Fields](docs/design-system.md#one-order-for-a-pull-requests-fields)).
