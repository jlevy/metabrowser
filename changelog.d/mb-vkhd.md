- **A stack member’s place leads its title in the Pulls tab.** A pull request in a stack
  shows its place in bold right after its state icon, then a small gray dot, then its
  title, as in “14/15 · Fix the reader”, instead of before its number on the line under
  the title. A pull request in no stack shows its title right after the icon
  ([Stacks](docs/design-system.md#stacks)).
