- The Treemap names far more of its cells: each cell’s icon is sized to the cell, so a
  cell too small for its name shows a smaller icon rather than none, down to a 4 px
  glyph, and an icon beside a name follows the name’s size, at most 12 px.
  On this repository at 1400 by 900 the view now names 98% of its cells by Files, from
  2%, and half by Bytes, from 15%. See
  [Folder Treemap](docs/design-system.md#folder-treemap).
