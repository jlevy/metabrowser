- The [Folder Treemap](docs/design-system.md#folder-treemap) goes as deep as it has room
  for, instead of stopping at two levels: small folders nest inside a thin frame with no
  label, every cell names itself in its tooltip, and the cell budget spreads across the
  map by area. The Treemap asks for the deepest and widest tree the rollup serves.
  Cells on the map’s right and bottom edges are no longer clipped by its border.
