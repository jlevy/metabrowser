- A stack’s page in a narrow pane now tells its state badges apart by their words: a
  tally of all four states leaves out each badge’s “Pulls”, “12 Merged”, where every
  badge was cut to “12 Pull…”, and a badge or an author’s name cut short says itself in
  its tooltip ([The Stack Page](docs/design-system.md#the-stack-page)).

- A stack whose comparison was kept without a size, by an earlier version or after a
  measurement ran out of time, now gets its size on the next read, instead of only after
  a member moves.
