- The Overview shows a compact treemap under File Overview’s type breakdown, following
  its measure, Show ignored, and legend hover.
  Its outer box is the folder itself: clicking it opens the Treemap view at the folder,
  and clicking a cell inside opens the Treemap view at that folder, or at the folder
  holding a file.
- The Treemap view draws the same outer box for the folder, labelled with its name, so
  the two maps of a folder match.
