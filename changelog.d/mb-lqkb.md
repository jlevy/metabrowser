- **A Pulls row shows its number and lines changed after its title.** A row reads “Fix
  the reader · #455 · +1,234 −567 ›”, with the author and the age on the line below, and
  no longer counts files added, deleted, and modified; a stack’s heading drops its file
  counts too. A click on the lines or the chevron after them opens or closes the pull
  request’s changed files; a click anywhere else on the row opens its page
  ([The Pulls Tab](docs/design-system.md#the-pulls-tab)).
