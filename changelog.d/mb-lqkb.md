- **A Pulls row shows the lines its pull request changes.** A row shows “+1,234 −567 ›”
  and no longer counts files added, deleted, and modified; a stack’s heading drops its
  file counts too. A click on the lines or the chevron after them opens or closes the
  pull request’s changed files; a click anywhere else on the row opens its page
  ([The Pulls Tab](docs/design-system.md#the-pulls-tab)).
