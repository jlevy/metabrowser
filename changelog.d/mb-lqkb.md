- **A Pulls row’s third line holds its number, author, and lines changed.** The line
  under a row’s title reads “#455 · octo · +1,234 −567”, with the age at its end, and no
  longer counts files added, deleted, and modified; a stack’s heading drops its file
  counts too. A click anywhere on that line, the number and the author included, now
  opens or closes the pull request’s changed files instead of opening its page; a click
  on the title still opens the page
  ([The Pulls Tab](docs/design-system.md#the-pulls-tab)).
