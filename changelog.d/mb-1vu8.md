- **The Commits tab has the branch chooser, and shares it with Files.** A repository
  opened by its URL, or a folder that is a repository’s root, shows one revision row
  under the Files and Commits tabs, and the Commits graph is the history of the branch,
  tag, or commit chosen there, as Files shows its files; on a folder’s working tree it
  is `HEAD`, its upstream, and the trunks, as before.
  A revision chosen on the Commits tab reloads the page on Commits at its newest commit,
  and a page left on a revision no longer served offers to reload on either tab
  ([Browsing History](docs/browsing-history.md#the-commits-tab)).
