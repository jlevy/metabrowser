- **A longer conventional-commit scope is set apart too.** A commit’s or pull request’s
  title such as `feat(view-on-github): …` now draws its prefix at regular weight and the
  rest bold, as a short prefix does: a prefix counts up to 32 characters, where it
  counted up to 16. A parenthetical sentence before a colon is still part of the title
  ([Titles](docs/design-system.md#titles)).
