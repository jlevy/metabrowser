- The agent skill’s **Write a Guided Review** asks for shorter guides: a description
  that opens with three to five lines on the end state, about eight steps or fewer, one
  for each area, notes of one to three sentences, and a stacked layer’s place in one
  line. A guide cites what a reviewer must read rather than every changed file, and a
  refresh no longer adds a step for each file a push added.
