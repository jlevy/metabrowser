- **The macOS app’s plugin list opens with a click on its name.** It sat behind a
  disclosure under Settings > Information, where a click on the word Plugins did
  nothing, since a disclosure on macOS opens only from its arrow.
  It is Settings > Plugins now: each plugin’s name, ID, version, source, and the plugins
  it requires, then any discovery errors, read when the tab first shows and again on
  Refresh ([Settings](docs/macos-app.md#settings)).
