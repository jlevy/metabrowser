- A Guided Review’s step list no longer indents a row that is in no group.
  The overview’s title, and a step outside any group, start on the edge the group
  headings start on, so the overview no longer sits out alone at the titles’ column
  under no heading, in a guide with groups or without.
  A group’s steps keep their numbered column under its heading
  ([Guided Review](docs/design-system.md#guided-review)).
