- On a pull request’s page, each pull request in the stack button’s menu is now one
  line: its state icon and its title, cut short with the whole title in its tooltip,
  then at the line’s end its place in the stack in bold and its number, such as “14/15 ·
  #455”. The menu no longer checks the current pull request, which keeps its highlight
  ([Stacks](docs/design-system.md#stacks)).
