- A pull request’s page no longer has a Refresh of its own: the navigation’s Refresh
  refreshes it, with the rest of what the page shows.
  Where the page has not fetched the pull request, or its last fetch or refresh failed,
  its status line offers Fetch or Retry, which work while the navigation pane is hidden
  ([Refresh, Retry, and Reload Page](docs/design-system.md#refresh-retry-and-reload-page)).
