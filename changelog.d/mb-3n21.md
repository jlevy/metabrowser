- **Each open pull request’s readiness in the Pulls tab and on stacks.** A Pulls row’s
  last line has a column between its lines and its age with the verdict’s icon in its
  color, green when nothing blocks the pull request, yellow while it waits, red when its
  author must change something, and, where the tab is wide enough, the verdict’s words
  after it, such as “Merge conflicts”; a screen reader hears them after the row’s state.
  A stack’s member, in the stack menu and on the stack’s page, ends its line with the
  same icon. A closed or merged pull request shows none, and so does an open one GitHub
  has not yet been asked about for its head.
  The tab reads every row’s verdict from the cache in one request, and again when the
  scan of every open pull request’s status starts or ends, or a row’s head or state
  moves; the column takes a little of the narrowest tab’s room, where a row’s lines may
  give way to an ellipsis
  ([The Pulls Tab](docs/reviewing-pull-requests.md#the-pulls-tab),
  [A Pull Request’s Readiness](docs/design-system.md#in-the-pulls-tab-and-on-stacks)).
