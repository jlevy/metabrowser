- **Guided Review shows each step whole, and nothing in it moves.** A step appears in
  one change, once its text, its excerpts, its documents’ rendered layers, and on the
  overview the description are drawn, so the step list no longer drops in above the
  description, a step’s card no longer empties and grows back, and a document no longer
  shows its source before its rendering.
  The step a reader leaves stays on screen until the next one replaces it.

- **Guided Review reads ahead of the reader.** A pull request’s page reads its guide’s
  first step while the reader is on another tab, a stack’s page while Docs or Changes
  shows, and the guide reads the steps next to the one shown, so opening the tab or
  moving one step shows the step at once.
  What a guide has read is kept while the page is open, so leaving the tab and coming
  back reads nothing again ([Guided Review](docs/design-system.md#guided-review)).
