- **Chips show whether they are clickable.** A chip you can click, a pull request’s
  readiness badge, a branch or tag chip, a table’s reference, or the chip that stands
  for an image a document does not load, shows the pointer and takes a darker ground
  under it, or a lighter one in the dark theme, and is never underlined: the readiness
  badge and an image’s chip underlined on hover, and a table reference’s ground matched
  its row’s. A static chip, a state, a check, a label, or a tag, shows nothing under the
  pointer, and a front-matter error’s badge no longer shows the help cursor
  ([Clickable and Static Chips](docs/design-system.md#clickable-and-static-chips)).
